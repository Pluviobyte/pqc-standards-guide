Understanding Quantum Resistant Cryptography Standards in 2026

NIST finalized post-quantum cryptography standards in 2024. Organizations are transitioning from RSA and ECC to quantum-resistant alternatives.

CRYSTALS-Kyber (ML-KEM)

Selected as the primary key encapsulation mechanism with three security levels: Kyber-512, Kyber-768, and Kyber-1024, based on the Module Learning With Errors problem.

CRYSTALS-Dilithium (ML-DSA)

For digital signatures, provides strong security with compact sizes. Adopted by AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.

Implementation Timeline

NIST recommends completing assessments by 2025 and full deployment by 2030.
